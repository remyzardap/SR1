# Investor Summary

Use this skill when the user wants an investment angle on a company, asset, or market.

## Steps

1. Search for the company or asset and read the most recent financial/operational sources.
2. Identify: business model, revenue drivers, growth metrics, valuation signal, and 2-3 key risks.
3. Write a concise memo: Thesis, Metrics, Risks, Recommendation.
4. Cite every claim with `[n]` markers and append source cards.

## Output format

- **Thesis**: 1-2 sentences.
- **Key metrics**: 3-5 bullets.
- **Risks**: 2-3 bullets.
- **Recommendation**: Buy / Hold / Watch / Avoid with reasoning.
- **Sources**: numbered cards.

## Rules

- Distinguish facts from interpretation.
- Do not provide personalized financial advice.
- Always disclose when data is estimated or incomplete.
