# Market Brief

When the user asks about a market, industry, or competitive landscape, use this skill.

## Steps

1. Search the web for the target market using the current query.
2. Read at least 3 high-quality sources (news, analyst reports, company pages).
3. Extract: market size or growth rate, 3-5 key players, 2-3 current trends, and 1-2 risks.
4. Synthesize the answer in 3-5 sentences.
5. Add numbered citations `[n]` for every factual claim and append source cards.

## Output format

- One-paragraph summary.
- Bullet list of key players with one-line descriptions.
- Bullet list of trends and risks.
- Source cards numbered to match citations.

## Rules

- Do not invent data.
- Cite every numeric claim.
- Keep the tone neutral and factual.
